# Edition 5 recovery-to-porting handoff

Prepared 2026-10-19 for [#37](https://github.com/Kurak0oo/bomb-it-5/issues/37). The selected Normal Arcade/Cinema structure is now explained by source, bytecode and controlled original-runtime cases. The current Godot game remains a playable reconstruction with specific approximations. These findings have **not** been ported by this research work. This report identifies differences and an ordered implementation path; it does not close a tracker gate, certify release readiness or assign a fidelity percentage.

## Authority and evidence strength

Authority is the unchanged publisher SWF, SHA256 `718c04c741604bda2bcf4efb19ca3f7cf240b79bc6163f274045c3157e0408a2`. ActionScript paths below are relative to `.scratch/edition-5-research/publisher-scripts/scripts/zlong/bombit5/`. [Structure reconstruction](edition-5-structure-reconstruction.md) owns startup, ownership, phases and bytecode corroboration; [the inventory](edition-5-structure-inventory.json) pins source/timeline identities. The inspected `scripts/` implementation is native GDScript; it does not embed the original ActionScript runtime.

Reference playback uses pinned Ruffle `45366370ed7d1a59c89aa3cf7b765c7a274407cd`. Each scenario manifest identifies its actual preserved executable and research patches. A later combined build does not retroactively become an earlier scenario's executable. [Reference session](edition-5-reference-session.md), [event limitations](edition-5-reference-events.md) and [trace design](edition-5-reference-trace-design.md) distinguish legal input, passive metadata and original-method fixtures. Fixtures invoke original constructors/controllers/callbacks with their guards; they are not naturally achieved input-only gameplay or raw outcome writes.

This is original-SWF execution through that emulator, **not a native Adobe Flash comparison**. Date/getTimer/Tween overhead and live array/dictionary mutation matter. Whole-session collector ON/OFF equivalence failed; seeded OFF repeats also diverged. Later bounded comparisons succeed for their stated fields: 105 overlap frames and the AI return-hook opening comparison. These do not establish global determinism or invisible instrumentation. Source counters, callback counts, harness frames, movie frames and host milliseconds remain separate units.

The existing **244 Godot checks are functional checks**, covering command/scheduler/round, scene and full-board playthrough behavior. They are not 244 paired original-reference assertions. This handoff inspects production code and evidence; it does not claim a new Godot test run or a completed paired replay suite.

## Accepted changes to preserve

[DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) and [ADR0005](../adr/0005-integrate-gameplay-through-the-round.md) remain authoritative for intentional changes:

- External 35 Hz scheduling, atomic fixed-roster command admission, ordered down/up batches, fixed actor priority, five-tick bounded catch-up, uniform newborn-bomb exclusion and accepted deterministic phases.
- Equal-speed eight-direction human/AI movement, full collision footprint, wall sliding, blocked-corner prevention, corridor assistance within movement distance, and departure from an overlapping bomb until fully clear.
- A 180-second simulation clock, paused simulation time, timeout loss while enemies remain, human-loss priority over simultaneous elimination, and elimination priority over timeout when enemies are gone.
- Focus-loss pause, explicit resume and fresh input; no stale held/queued actions. Original pending Tween completion during pause and resume-Space firing do not replace that policy.
- The fixed recorded CinemaStage1 instance and retry/play-again on that board. Original template generation and level progression do not add a generator or campaign.
- Readable labels, the functional fixed visible stick, deferred phone/iOS evaluation, four unresolved named item slots, and exclusion of copied source sprites/music/art from production.

Source state order, partial vehicle-list mutation, mine underflow and parser defects are **not separately approved corrections**. Later work should preserve selected behavior or record a deliberate difference through the existing design/frontier process. The unrelated Hot Pursuit allocation is a bytecode-confirmed source artifact, not a gameplay feature to recreate.

## Ordered faithful-port work

1. **Freeze the comparison contract around the real round.** Keep the playable build and 244 functional checks as a regression baseline. Use `TickScheduler.set_direction/enqueue_bomb_event/advance_time` with the real `BombSimulation` target, then inspect returned tick results/`snapshot`. Use a small scene layer for input, display and session flow. Record source case hash, actual initial board/actors/equipment, input-cutoff mapping, random consumption and clock category. A source-generated board can be a declared comparison fixture without replacing the accepted production CSV. Do not assume seed equality or derive original expectations from current Godot output.
2. **Recover lifecycle ownership behind that seam.** Model actor/control/action/hazard responsibilities inside `BombSimulation.advance:71`, retaining copied public snapshots and atomic rejection. Separate delivery, interruption, completion/replacement, protection, physical/logical occupancy and dead/removed/result state. Translate original registration/mutation into the accepted phases with explicit correspondence; introduce no second simulation clock or parallel test-only engine.
3. **Port the player's observable feel first.** Work ordinary fire/contact/capacity and chain overlap, then hammer/laser/mine, followed by flamer geometry and triple/fireball emission/collision/recovery. Use actual source action/frame/target cases and attachment/bounds metadata as reference. Keep original artwork in research. For host-clock Tween behavior document a simulation-time conversion consistent with accepted pause; verify ordering and independent projectile lifetime rather than forcing every observed host run into one nominal duration. Add a public behavioral regression for each recovered distinction before its implementation change.
4. **Port protection, drops and result accounting.** Apply distinct stages to ride loss, shield/Invincibility, collection/target eligibility, drop reveal/quota/shared list, death/results and retry hearts. Keep unresolved draws empty. Preserve separate HUD awards and final result formula; exercise zero-kill win, simultaneous defeat and one-time settlement through public results. Do not spend hearts at initial damage while describing it as source parity.
5. **Port Normal AI through the same command producer.** Replace BFS heuristics with recovered state/control/action/search responsibilities behind `AIController.command`, without private AI position/damage powers. Keep shared movement/collision. Use perception/RNG/path cases and stop/death/transfer registration to explain decisions; explicitly map cardinal route intent onto accepted eight-direction physical capability. Preserve observed source quirks or document deliberate corrections instead of silently changing target sampling/priority.
6. **Repeat aligned desktop assessment, then presentation/release work.** Replay the real scheduler/round scenarios with accepted differences labeled; report the first differing public event and its explanation. Revisit the user's bomb/weapon feel observations. Once gameplay fidelity work is accepted, finish original redraw/animation under #20 and integrated desktop acceptance under #21. Physical phone/iOS testing and joystick polish follow as already agreed.

Internal modules can preserve original responsibilities without recreating every ActionScript class or unused allocation. If another meaningful observation is needed, expose it through the existing read-only round snapshot, rather than mutable testing backdoors. Tests should observe public behavior rather than mirror private helpers. [The existing runner](../../tools/run_checks.ps1) remains the functional baseline; paired evidence requires aligned new scenarios, not renaming its assertions.

## Gate assessment and bounded remaining work

Earlier material unknowns now have explanations: source fire persists after detonation; cleanup and visible flames can diverge under overlap; flamer geometry is nested/animated; weapon recovery is not one cooldown; AI quirks execute; death/hearts/results have separate stages. The final target/lifecycle reports explain selected interactions and paused pending recovery, including genuine replacement versus a pending pickup destroyed by the active flamer. Current Godot mismatches are **implementation work**, rather than evidence that the original remains fundamentally unexplained.

No new material original-behavior contradiction was identified by this read-only audit. Remaining unexhausted cases include arbitrary morph/filter/skin/ride transforms, every target/direction/interleaving, successful opportunistic AI turn-and-hit, all late replacement/round-end callback orders, native Flash comparison and audio playback. These bound the claims; they do not require exhausting the retail campaign or every possible state before a selected-scope port can begin. A newly observed unexplained selected-scope failure would become a concrete recovery blocker.
