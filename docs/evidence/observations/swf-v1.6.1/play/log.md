1. Hold Left, press Up, release Up: NOT TESTED (no way to hold keys with the tooling).
2. Hold Space: NOT TESTED (same reason). Single Space press placed exactly one bomb; stats show 1 bomb capacity at start.
3. Fuse time: only one measured trial. Bomb present at 36.57, blast at 36.81; bomb placed shortly before 35.27 (+ unknown tool latency) -> estimated roughly 2-3 s (inferred, low confidence).
4. Blast through soft blocks: NOT conclusively tested; in 18c the blast cross appeared to extend only through empty cells, soft blocks in the arenas were destroyed by AI blasts over time (inferred only).

Lag: no frame stutter noticed in Ruffle; the main delay was tool latency (~8-12 s per action). Not captured: item pickup by me, soft-block breaking by my bomb in a still frame, victory screen, hold-key probes.
